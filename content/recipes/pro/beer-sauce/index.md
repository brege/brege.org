## Beer Sauce

**Chefs:** Ryan Fleener, Wyatt Brege



### Ingredients

-   3 qt Mayonaisse
-   12 fl oz Guiness (bottle)
-   3 Tbsp Coarse Grind Black Pepper

### Directions 

Add ingredients to a large mixing bowl and whisk.
